# Prototype: CDMS Post-Auction Menus (Customer Inquiry, Sales Actions, Report, Approval, FAD, SMD, VWD, Settings), JBA Philippines

> **Generated**: 2026-10-09 via /logique-b1-prototyping; updated 2026-10-16 for JBAP's feedback of 16 Oct 2026 (items 1 to 23 and Customer Transaction History); updated 2026-10-19 for the round-2 proposals (Q69 to Q80, Q82) and JBAP's feedback of 19 Oct 2026; updated 2026-10-20 for JBAP's answers to the open questions of 12 Oct 2026 (Q5 rebate approval, Q11 SAAF, Q14 holiday list, Q21 customer page tabs) and for the user request that moved Rebate Payable to SMD (new SMD › Rebate menu; JBAP reversed it on 26 Oct 2026); updated 2026-10-26 for JBAP's feedback of 26 Oct 2026 (round 5: SMD › Result Price with Cancel sale, Approval › Rebate Approval, Rebate Monitoring and Liability to Seller in Report, Rebate Payable back in FAD, FAD › Vehicle Dispatch, Document Monitoring with locked dates and two emails, Sales Actions, editable email bodies)
> **Scope**: Clickable to-be prototype of the new FAD, SMD and VWD menus in CDMS (plus Customer Inquiry, Sales Actions, Report, Approval and Settings › Holiday List) that replace the MS Access post-auction forms (frmFAD, frmSMD, frmVWD). Built from the approved screen designs and the CR form. SMD › Result Price is a simulated screen: it stands in for the Result Price module that CDMS already has.
> **Status**: Prototype, not production code

## How to open

**Double-click `index.html`.** That's it: no server and no setup needed.

The prototype is plain HTML, CSS and JavaScript. Data sits in `data.js` and is loaded with a `<script>` tag, so it works straight from the folder (no `fetch()`). `xlsx.js` is a small built-in Excel writer for downloadable reports (no library).

Sample data for the proposed improvements and the wish-list screens sits in `data-extra.js` (loaded right after `data.js`), together with the [MOCK] data of JBAP's feedback of 19 Oct 2026 (new vehicles from inspection, earlier listings of four units) and of 20 Oct 2026 (the holiday list), and of 26 Oct 2026 (rebate states on six lots, sales actions). The header switch **Proposed improvements** turns on LOGIQUE's proposals P3 to P6 (P1 was withdrawn after JBAP's feedback of 15 Oct 2026: JBAP keeps the Access format for Buyer Paid; P2 was withdrawn after JBAP's feedback of 26 Oct 2026, because cancellations are made in SMD › Result Price; P5 shrank on 20 Oct 2026, because JBAP made the SAAF standard: see *Proposed improvements*); a link ending in `?proposed=1` (for example `index.html?proposed=1`) opens with it on.

**Works in** Chrome, Edge, Firefox and Safari on Windows, macOS or Linux. If double-click does not open a browser: right-click `index.html` > Open With > choose a browser.

## What is clickable

- **Only Customer › Customer Inquiry and Sales Actions, Report, Approval, FAD, SMD, VWD, Settings › Holiday List and Log Activity are active.** All other CDMS menus (Dashboard, Cars, Payment, etc.) are disabled and show a short message when clicked.
- **39 screens**: Customer 2 (**Customer Inquiry** and **Sales Actions**), Report 7, Approval 1 (**Rebate Approval**, a new top menu for the President, right after Report), Auction 1 (**Result Price**, the existing CDMS screen), FAD 14, SMD 8, VWD 4, Settings 1 (**Holiday List**, below Staff), Log Activity 1 (last item in the sidebar), plus a Home page (cards for FAD, SMD, VWD and Report, with counts per list) and a customer page per buyer. **All 39 screens show with the Proposed improvements switch off**: the SMD **Seller Awarding Acknowledgement** screen is standard since 20 Oct 2026 (JBAP answer Q11); it used to show only with the switch on. FAD's **Commission Billing**, **Billing Monitoring** and **Unpaid Billing** are three tabs of one **Billing** menu entry, FAD's **For Dispatch** and **Return to Seller Approval** are two tabs of one **Vehicle Dispatch** menu entry (JBAP, 26 Oct 2026), and SMD's **Return to Seller** and **SMD Manager Approval** are two tabs of one **Return to Seller** menu entry. **SMD › Result Price** (JBAP, 26 Oct 2026) is the first SMD entry: SMD types the rebate there and cancels a sale there; FAD has no **Cancellation Entry** any more. **Rebate Payable** is a FAD menu entry again (JBAP, 26 Oct 2026; SMD held it from 20 Oct 2026) and SMD has no Rebate menu any more. **Report** holds **Result Summary**, **Auction Monitoring**, **Inventory Monitoring**, **Inventory Summary**, **Cancellation Monitoring**, **Rebate Monitoring** and **Liability to Seller**: Result Summary, Auction Monitoring and Cancellation Monitoring left FAD and SMD, Inventory Monitoring left VWD and SMD (JBAP, 16 and 19 Oct 2026), and Rebate Monitoring and Liability to Seller left FAD (JBAP, 26 Oct 2026). There is no SMD Inventory and no VWD Inventory Monitoring any more. **Old links to the moved or removed screens still work and open the new screen**, and the address bar shows the new link; the five links of 26 Oct 2026 are `#/FAD/rebate-monitoring` and `#/SMD/rebate-monitoring` (open Report › Rebate Monitoring), `#/SMD/rebate-payable` (opens FAD › Rebate Payable), `#/FAD/liability-to-seller` (opens Report › Liability to Seller) and `#/FAD/cancellation-entry` (opens Report › Cancellation Monitoring).
- Every menu item in the sidebar shows its **number of records** (FAD › Billing, FAD › Vehicle Dispatch and SMD › Return to Seller show a count on each of their tabs instead; Inventory Summary counts its sellers; Approval › Rebate Approval counts the rebates waiting for the President; with the Proposed improvements switch on, the P6 badges replace the number; Log Activity has none).
- **New badge** (26 Oct 2026): everything added or changed after JBAP's feedback of 26 Oct 2026 carries a blue **New** badge: menu entries and Home links, page titles and group tabs, column headers, buttons and the Edit button of every email preview. Hover a badge for its meaning (*Added or changed after JBAP's feedback of 26 Oct 2026*). It is one badge, always on (not tied to the Proposed improvements switch; the amber **Proposed P#** tags stay separate), and it is hidden when printing. The badges of the 19 and 20 Oct 2026 changes were removed on 26 Oct 2026. These carry it: the screens **SMD › Result Price**, **Approval › Rebate Approval**, **FAD › Vehicle Dispatch** (the menu entry and both tabs), **FAD › Document Monitoring**, **FAD › Rebate Payable**, **Report › Rebate Monitoring**, **Report › Liability to Seller** and **Customer › Sales Actions**; the **Sales Actions** tab of the customer page; the columns **Rebate (approved)** and **Rebate Status** on FAD › Pending Awarding and **For DOAS Email?** and **Change Date** on Document Monitoring; the buttons **Email DOAS for signature** and **Print document transmittal**; and the **Edit** button in every email preview. The test `tests/round5-badges.test.js` pins this list, so a badge cannot appear or stay on anything else without the test failing.
- **Dropdown filters are multi-select** (JBAP, 19 Oct 2026; JBAP wrote "radio type", we read it as tick boxes: Q83). A dropdown filter (Seller, Status, Parking Location …) shows **All** or the ticked values ("EWB, SBC"; "N selected" from three). Open it with a click, or Enter or Space on its button; tick one or more values; press **Apply filter** at the bottom of the dropdown (or Apply in the filter form); the same bar has **Reset**. **Escape**, tabbing out of the list or a click outside closes it (the ticks stay until you apply or reset); **Reset** clears every filter. A row passes when it matches **any** ticked value, and all filters together as before. Text and date-range filters are unchanged. A default such as Document Monitoring › Status = Open, or Rebate Approval › Rebate Status = Pending approval, arrives already ticked. At 1366px or narrower an open dropdown can cover the form's own Reset and Apply buttons, so both are also inside the dropdown (Reset, then Apply filter). After Apply or Reset, keyboard focus returns to the dropdown's button, or to the form's Apply filter button when it was not in a dropdown.
- Every list has working **filters, column sorting, page size and paging**, an empty state, and totals where the design has them.
- **Dates can be typed** in every editable date cell and in the date fields of forms: `10/07/2026`, `10/07/26`, `10/07`, `1007`, `2026-10-07`, or **`t` for today** (the demo date). The calendar button next to the date still opens the date picker. A date that cannot be read stays in the box, turns red and is reported when you save. Filter date ranges keep the browser's picker.

## Flow walkthrough (FAD, data flows between lists and into VWD)

1. **Auction › Result Price** (the existing CDMS screen *Set Result Price*, under the Auction menu; JBAP, 26 and 27 Oct 2026): laid out like CDMS (Update, Auction Status, Auction No., Lot No., Model, Plate Number, Seller, Buyer ID, Buyer Name, Start Price, Minimum Bid Price, Highest Offer Price, Winner Confirmation, Offer Status, KYC Status, Justification Log; filters Seller, Auction No., Lot No., Plate No., Auction Status). It lists every lot with an auction status, newest auction first. The fields added for JBAP carry the New badge: **Rebate**, **Rebate Status** and the **Auction Status** choice *Cancelled*. Columns the sample data does not hold (Start Price, Winner Confirmation, Offer Status, KYC Status) stay blank. On a sold lot that is not released, type a **Rebate** (0 or more, not above the Result Price, blank = none; read-only once the lot has an Awarding Date) > **Update** on that row: Rebate Status becomes *Pending approval*, the request date is today, the lot waits for the President (step 2) and the change is logged under module Auction. Typing another amount on a rebate that was already approved or rejected sends it back to *Pending approval*; typing the same amount again changes nothing (unless the rebate has no status yet, as on Access lots); clearing the box removes the rebate. Setting Auction Status to *Cancelled* is step 10. (Until 27 Oct this was a simulated SMD › Result Price; that link now opens Auction › Result Price.)
2. **Approval › Rebate Approval** (JBAP, 26 Oct 2026; a new top menu for the President, right after Report): the list opens on the rebates waiting for a decision (Rebate Status = *Pending approval*; tick *Approved* or *Rejected* as well to see the history) with Auction No., Lot, Plate Number, Make/Model, Seller, Customer Name, Result Price, Rebate, Requested, Rebate Status, Decided and Reason. **Approve** (asks to confirm) sets *Approved* and today's date; **Reject** asks for a **Reason** (required) and sets *Rejected*, today's date and the reason. The two buttons show on pending rows only, a rebate is decided once, and each decision is logged under module *Approval*. The menu number is the count of pending rebates. Sample: 3 pending, 2 approved and 1 rejected rebate (see *Seeded test data*).
3. **Pending Awarding**: tick *For BAAF* on 1 or more lots (optionally change Awarding Date or Security Bond) > **Email BAAF** > preview one email per buyer > **Send**. Lots move to **Unpaid**. **Rebate (approved)** and **Rebate Status** are read-only (JBAP, 26 Oct 2026): the amount is the rebate the President approved, and it is blank while the rebate is pending or after it was rejected (a rejected rebate means no rebate). A lot whose rebate is *Pending approval* cannot be awarded: with an Awarding Date entered, Save changes says *The rebate is waiting for the President's approval.*, and **Email BAAF** holds such lots back with the same message (it stays above the list and the lot's Awarding Date cell turns red) and sends the others. The BAAF's rebate attachment lists the approved amount only. Round 4's editable *Rebate* and *Rebate Approved (President)* columns are gone: FAD no longer types a rebate or an approval date. **Seller Approved** shows the date SMD recorded on the SAAF screen (Q11). Sample: V102 and V095 have an approved rebate, V092, V067 and V074 a pending one, V082 a rejected one.
4. **Unpaid**: enter *Buyer Paid* (type `t` for today, the demo date, or use the calendar button; the row is marked unsaved) > **Save changes**. Lot moves to **Payment Seller**. Try a future date, or `13/45`, to see validation.
5. **Payment Seller**: tick *Print Payment to Seller* > **Record payment date & print** (JBAP, 19 Oct 2026, FAD #1). The dialog shows the report per seller and asks one **Payment date** (type it, or `t` for today; default today; required, not in the future, not before the latest Buyer Paid of the printed lots) > **Record payment date & print**. That date is recorded as *Payment to Seller* on every printed lot (and printed on the reports), the print ticks are cleared, any other unsaved edit on those rows (such as the Payment Voucher No. with the switch on) is saved, the changes are logged, and the browser's print dialog opens. The lots leave the list for **Seller Acknowledgement** and **Billing › Commission Billing**. A date typed in the *Payment to Seller Date* cell and saved still works for a lot that was not printed. Six sample lots (V128 to V133) start with the print tick set.
6. **Seller Acknowledgement**: enter the date and save. **Vehicle Dispatch** is one menu entry with two tabs, For Dispatch and Return to Seller Approval (JBAP, 26 Oct 2026; the rules, columns and actions of both are unchanged). **For Dispatch** lists lots whose seller condition is met (Buyer Payment / DOAS sellers after Buyer Paid, others after Seller Acknowledgement): enter the date and save. Dispatched lots appear in **Unreleased Unit** (FAD), in SMD **Unreleased Unit** (the same live list, view only; JBAP, 19 Oct 2026, SMD #1) and in VWD **Unreleased Unit / Unreleased Unit (Overdue)**. VWD can then click **Release vehicle** on a row (see *Reports and emails*). A lot with a storage penalty cannot be released: on FAD **Unreleased Unit** press **Record payment** (optional Reference / OR No.). The payment is recorded as paid today, the dispatch deadline becomes today, the lot leaves VWD *Unreleased Unit (Overdue)* and can be released (JBAP, 16 Oct 2026, #9). After a payment the penalty starts again the next day, PHP 500 a day, until the unit is released (Q84). **Extend** (a button on every row; proposal Q74, 19 Oct 2026) opens a dialog with the customer, the current deadline and the penalty due, and asks for a typed **New deadline** (after the current deadline), a **Reason** and **Approved by (FAD Manager)**, all required. The new date becomes the dispatch deadline, so the penalty is recalculated from it and VWD sees the lot in the right list; the change is logged (Dispatch Deadline, Extension Reason, Extension Approved By) and kept in the lot's `deadline_changes`.
7. **Document Monitoring** (JBAP, 26 Oct 2026): the list holds the sold lots JBAP has paid to the seller (*Payment to Seller* filled in) and, for **EWB**, whose buyers sign the DOAS at EWB's HO or store, the lots the buyer has paid (*Buyer Paid*); TFS lots stay (the DOAS columns show n/a); cancelled lots are not listed. The sample has 104 lots, 98 in the Open view. The columns follow JBAP's Documents Monitoring sheet. **Status** is the furthest step reached: *Pending DOAS*, *DOAS for Signing*, *Pending Documents*, *For Releasing* or *Complete* (there is no *Pending Payment* and no *Cancelled* any more); the Status filter opens on *Open*, which hides Complete lots. DOAS Aging and Docs Aging count working days while a step waits and update by themselves.
   - **Locked dates**: a date that is saved is shown as text and cannot be typed over; a blank date is still typed (`t` is today). The text cells (EWB HO/Store, Remarks) stay editable. **Change date** (a button on every row that has a saved date) opens a dialog: pick the **Field** (the row's filled dates), type the **New date** and give a **Reason** (required). The new date gets the same checks as the list (not in the future, not before Buyer Paid, steps in order) and so do the dates that depend on it: a change that would put a later date out of order is refused and names that date (for example *JBAP Receipt of Signed DOAS (09/30/2026) cannot be before Receipt of DOAS (10/08/2026).*). A saved change logs the old and new date and the reason (field *Change reason*), and the status and aging recalculate.
   - **Email DOAS for signature**: the tick **For DOAS Email?** is open on lots whose DOAS is signed at JBAP, with Receipt of DOAS blank and status Pending DOAS (not EWB, not TFS). Tick lots > **Email DOAS for signature** > preview one email per buyer (From finance@jbap.com.ph, To the buyer, Cc sales@jbap.com.ph, subject `JBAP DOAS FOR SIGNATURE - <BUYER NAME>`, a table of Seller, Buyer, Year, Make, Model and Plate Number) > **Send**: Receipt of DOAS is today on those lots (logged) and the status becomes *DOAS for Signing*. EWB keeps its call-out flow (our reading, to confirm).
   - **Print Transmittal**: *Print Transmittal?* > **Print document transmittal** now also emails the buyer the **Documents Release** notice (one email per buyer; From finance@jbap.com.ph, To the buyer, Cc sales@jbap.com.ph, subject `JBAP DOCUMENTS RELEASE - <BUYER NAME>`, a table of Seller, Buyer, Year, Make, Model, Plate Number and Color) and records **JBAP Receipt of Documents** as today on the printed lots that have it blank (logged); the status becomes *For Releasing*. JBAP's sentence about this was cut off, so the email and the receipt date are our reading, to confirm. The dialog shows the transmittal form and the email preview. A first print of a lot with a blank JBAP Receipt of Documents needs at least one document ticked or Others typed, else the dialog stays open with a message under the lot. A lot that already has an ATF No. can be printed again as a **reprint**: same ATF No., no email and no new receipt date.
8. **Billing › Commission Billing** (columns Seller, Auction No. and Auction Date; JBAP, 16 and 19 Oct 2026, Q80): tick *Print?* for lots of **one seller** > **Print commission billing** > enter NOAH Billing No. and date > **Record billing & print**. A new billing appears in the *Billing Monitoring* and *Unpaid Billing* tabs.
9. **Billing › Unpaid Billing**: enter Payment Date + Collection Receipt > **Save**.
10. **Cancel a sale** (Auction › Result Price; JBAP, 26 Oct 2026; it replaces FAD › Cancellation Entry): set **Auction Status** to *Cancelled* on a sold lot that is not released and press **Update**. The dialog shows the lot and asks for the **Date Cancelled** (typed, default today; not in the future and not before the auction end), **Waived?** (Yes or No, default No) and a **Justification** (required when the fee is waived). **Cancel sale** (confirm) records the cancellation: penalty PHP 10,000 unless waived, split as in the rules table, status Cancelled, logged under module Auction (action Cancel). The lot shows as Cancelled in Result Price (its justification in the Justification Log column), leaves Document Monitoring and shows in **Report › Cancellation Monitoring** and, if the fee is not waived, in **FAD › Cancellation Billing & Payment**: tick *For Print?* > **Print cancellation billing**, then enter *Date Paid* (Amount is filled in) > **Save**; paid lots leave that list. FAD › Cancellation Entry and proposal P2 (CDMS cancellations waiting for FAD's waiver decision) no longer exist. Cancellation Payment no longer exists either (JBAP, 16 Oct 2026, #13, #14), and Cancellation Monitoring is **Report › Cancellation Monitoring** (step 16).
11. **FAD › Rebate Payable** (back in FAD on 26 Oct 2026; SMD held it from 20 Oct 2026): the list holds sold lots with an **approved** rebate, a Buyer Paid date and no Rebate Paid date; FAD enters *Rebate Paid* (not in the future, not before Buyer Paid) > **Save**; the lot leaves the list and shows the date as *Date Remitted* in Report › Rebate Monitoring. **Report › Rebate Monitoring** (moved from FAD on 26 Oct 2026; approved rebates only) > **Download report**: pick From and To auction > **Download .xlsx**. **Report › Liability to Seller** (moved from FAD on 26 Oct 2026): pick a date > **Generate**.
12. **Log Activity**: every change from steps 1 to 11 (including *Penalty Paid*, the new dispatch deadline and, for an extension, its reason and approver), every rebate SMD types in Result Price, every sale cancelled with Cancel sale, every rebate the President approves or rejects (module *Approval*), every date changed with Change date and its reason, every VWD release or return, every SMD Return to Seller clearance and every SMD and FAD Manager approval, every SAAF sent or approved, every Rebate Paid FAD records, every sales action added on a customer page (module SMD) and every holiday added or removed in Settings appears here as user `system`, one row per changed field with the old and new value (newest first). Filter by date, user, module (FAD, SMD, VWD, Approval, Settings), action or record/plate. Prints and emails that record a value (Awarding Date, Payment to Seller Date, Billing #, Transmittal Date, Dispatch Date, cancellation billing date and amount, vehicle release with type and checklist, documents received with Others/Remarks and ATF No., Receipt of DOAS, JBAP Receipt of Documents) are logged, and an email sent with an edited body also logs *Email body edited* (No to Yes) on its lots; previews that change nothing and *Generate* are not.
13. **Return to Seller** (SMD, wish 4): the list holds units with status *Not Sold* or *Cancelled* that are **not listed in an ongoing or future auction** (JBAP, 16 Oct 2026, #19; 17 units in the sample). The column **Number of Listing** (JBAP, 19 Oct 2026, SMD #2) shows how often the unit was listed: the auction lots with its plate, plus earlier listings outside the data ([MOCK] `prior_listings` on 4 units), plus 1 when it has been relisted. Tick *For Return* > **Print & email clearance form** > **Send & print** (the form's sign lines read *Approved in CDMS*). The SMD Manager and the FAD Manager then approve in CDMS (proposal Q76, 19 Oct 2026): the units wait in **SMD › Return to Seller › SMD Manager Approval** (type the *SMD Manager Approved* date; not in the future, not before the clearance), then in **FAD › Vehicle Dispatch › Return to Seller Approval** (type the *FAD Manager Approved* date; not in the future, not before the SMD approval); each approval is logged. Only FAD-approved units move to VWD **For Return to Seller** (with an *Approved by FAD Manager* column) > **Release vehicle** (type *Return to Seller*; the return date cannot be before the later of the clearance and the FAD approval). Sample: [MOCK] auction 222 (12 to 14 Oct 2026) holds three relisted units, which stay off the list.
14. **Report › Inventory Monitoring** (JBAP, 19 Oct 2026, VWD #4; replaces VWD *Inventory Monitoring* and SMD *Inventory*; wish 5, JBAP 16 Oct 2026, #2, #20, #23): **every unit in the yard**, one row each, for all departments: the **auction lots**, the **57 units of JBAP's parking file** (as of 12 Oct 2026) that are in the yard and are not lots, and **3 new vehicles from inspection** ([MOCK] sample; the real CDMS takes them from the Inspection module, which is not in the prototype). Columns: Date of Entry, VCN, Plate Number, CS No., Make/Model, Color, Year, Seller, Logistics, Originating Warehouse, Parking Location, Transfer Date, **First Auction Listed**, **End Date of Listing**, Auction Status, Inventory Status, Released to Buyer, Returned to Seller, Returned to Seller WH, Days in Yard. Filters (dropdowns are multi-select): *In yard / Out* (default In yard), *Auction Status*, *Inventory Status*, *Seller* (with *Not recorded*), *Parking Location* (OPEN, STEEL, IMET, *Not recorded*) and *Plate Number*; the *Latest auction* filter is gone. **View only**: *Inventory Status* is always calculated, first match wins: Released (Returned for a return to the seller), **For Return** (cleared by the SMD, even before the manager approvals), **For Release** (dispatched, waiting for the buyer's pull-out), **Awarded** (sold), **Listed** (the lot's own auction, or the auction it was relisted in, has not ended) and otherwise **Not Listed**; they show in the order Listed, Not Listed, Awarded, For Release, For Return, Released, Returned (JBAP's set, 4a to 4c). A unit from JBAP's file shows its file status (LISTED is Listed, FOR RETURN is For Return); a new inspection unit is Not Listed. The statuses Pending, For Next Auction and Storage and the VWD remarks are not built: JBAP's 19 Oct feedback replaced them with Listed / Not Listed (Q71, Q72). Yard and inspection units leave the auction columns blank.
15. **Security Bond** (FAD, JBAP feedback of 15 Oct 2026): lots with a bond appear once paid. Enter the ORCR and refund dates, Refund Via, or Forfeited. Tip: for Auction 220, Lot 119 (plate NYL9684), first enter Payment to Seller in FAD › Payment Seller (any date from 10/04 to 10/08/2026) so the lot appears in Document Monitoring (it lists paid lots only since 26 Oct 2026), then enter JBAP Receipt of Documents and Buyer Receipt of Documents there, any date from 10/04 to 10/08/2026, to see *Waiting ORCR*.
16. **Report** (JBAP, 16, 19 and 26 Oct 2026): **Result Summary** (Access snapshot, auctions 197 to 220), **Auction Monitoring** (live lots, with totals), **Inventory Monitoring** (step 14), **Inventory Summary**, **Cancellation Monitoring**, **Rebate Monitoring** and **Liability to Seller** (the last two moved from FAD on 26 Oct 2026, step 11). View, filter, sort only (Rebate Monitoring has Download report, Liability to Seller its date box). **Inventory Summary** (JBAP's main objective, 19 Oct 2026; proposal Q70, like the REPORT sheet of JBAP's parking file) counts the units in the yard, one row per seller: In Yard, Listed, Not Listed, Awarded, For Release and For Return, with a Total row. The *Parking Location* filter chooses the units counted (for example only OPEN PARKING); units without a seller are grouped as *Not recorded*. It reads the same rows as Inventory Monitoring, so the counts follow every change made in FAD, SMD and VWD. **Cancellation Monitoring** (proposal Q69) lists every cancellation, newest first: Auction No., Lot, Customer Name, Buyer ID, Plate Number, Seller, Date Cancelled, Waived?, Justification, Penalty, Billing Date, Date Paid and Cancellation Fee Remitted; filters Auction No., Customer Name / Buyer ID, Plate Number, Seller and Waived; totals the count and the penalty. Old links to FAD and SMD Cancellation Monitoring, to FAD Cancellation Entry and to VWD and SMD Inventory open the new screens. New cancellations are made with Auction Status Cancelled in Auction › Result Price (step 10).
17. **Customer Inquiry**: click a buyer to open the customer page. Its tabs are *Sold*, *Pending Awarding*, *Unpaid*, **Vehicle for release** (JBAP answer Q21, 20 Oct 2026: the buyer's sold lots with a Dispatch Date and no release yet, with the Sold columns plus Dispatch Date, Dispatch Deadline and Penalty), **Pending DOAS signature** (Receipt of DOAS filled, signed DOAS not back yet; since 19 Oct also **EWB lots that were called out and are not yet signed**, with an *EWB Call Out* column and *Working Days Since Receipt / Call Out*, counted from Receipt of DOAS, else from EWB Call Out), **Documents for release** (JBAP has the documents, the buyer does not yet), *Cancellation* (JBAP, 16 Oct 2026; Q82) and **Sales Actions** (JBAP, 26 Oct 2026; the last tab, marked New). Sample: buyer B9034 has two EWB lots called out on 24 Aug 2026 and not signed (auction 211 lot 122, auction 210 lot 125). The *Sold* tab has a new column **Date of Release / Return** (the release date of the lot, any release type, blank while the unit is in the yard). JBAP's feedback of 26 Oct 2026 asks again for the vehicles for release: that tab was built on 20 Oct 2026, so nothing changed. The **Sales Actions** tab (JBAP, 26 Oct 2026; the fields are our proposal) lists the buyer's actions, newest first (Date, Action, Notes, Next Follow-up, By), above an **Add sales action** form: **Date** (typed, default today, not in the future), **Action** (Call, Email, Visit, Meeting or Other), **Notes** (required) and **Next Follow-up** (optional, not before the Date). *By* is the signed-in user (prototype: *SMD user*). An added action goes to the top of the list, the tab and menu numbers go up, and it is logged under SMD (Create). Sample: B9034 has 4 actions, B0313 2 and B0193 1. **Customer › Sales Actions** lists every action with Date, Buyer ID, Customer Name, Action, Notes, Next Follow-up and By, newest first; filters Customer Name / Buyer ID, Action (multi-select), Date range and **Follow-up due** (Yes or No: Next Follow-up on or before today; B0313's action of 5 Oct is due on the demo date); it is view only, and a buyer's name or Buyer ID opens the customer page on its Sales Actions tab. The bulk vehicle inquiry (JBAP's Q21) is not built, by decision.
18. **Settings › Holiday List** (JBAP answer Q14, 20 Oct 2026; a new top menu below Staff): the days CDMS skips when it counts working days. ## Not simulated

- Persistence: all changes are lost on refresh (by design), including new Log Activity entries
- Real login: the header shows the CDMS user only, so every change made in the demo is logged as `system`. Log times use the demo date with the real clock time
- Real emails, NOAH and CDMS: email and print are previews; nothing is sent or posted. Auction › Result Price follows the layout of the existing CDMS screen; in the prototype it is built only to type rebates and cancel sales
- NOAH: JBAP's existing integration is not connected; NOAH numbers are typed (P3, Q43)
- Partial penalty payments (Q75): not built, a question for JBAP. The Sales Actions fields are our proposal (JBAP gave none). A penalty payment is recorded for the full amount due, as of the demo date. The SMD and FAD Manager approvals are typed dates: with no sign-in, the Activity Log shows `system`, not the approver. The three new vehicles from inspection are sample data (the Inspection module is not in the prototype)
- Holiday list: the 18 dates are sample data, not an official list (moving holidays are missing); the ADMIN role is not simulated, so every visitor can add or remove holidays, and the changes are lost on refresh. The President's rebate decision (Approve or Reject in Approval) is a button: with no sign-in every visitor can decide and the Activity Log shows `system`, not the approver; a sales action's *By* is the fixed text *SMD user*
- Pixel-perfect design: this is a prototype, not the final UI
