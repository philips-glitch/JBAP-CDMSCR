# Prototype: CDMS Post-Auction Menus (Customer Inquiry, Report, FAD, SMD, VWD, Settings), JBA Philippines

> **Generated**: 2026-10-09 via /logique-b1-prototyping; updated 2026-10-16 for JBAP's feedback of 16 Oct 2026 (items 1 to 23 and Customer Transaction History); updated 2026-10-19 for the round-2 proposals (Q69 to Q80, Q82) and JBAP's feedback of 19 Oct 2026; updated 2026-10-20 for JBAP's answers to the open questions of 12 Oct 2026 (Q5 rebate approval, Q11 SAAF, Q14 holiday list, Q21 customer page tabs)
> **Scope**: Clickable to-be prototype of the new FAD, SMD and VWD menus in CDMS (plus Customer Inquiry, Report and Settings › Holiday List) that replace the MS Access post-auction forms (frmFAD, frmSMD, frmVWD). Built from the approved screen designs and the CR form.
> **Status**: Prototype, not production code

## How to open

**Double-click `index.html`.** That's it: no server and no setup needed.

The prototype is plain HTML, CSS and JavaScript. Data sits in `data.js` and is loaded with a `<script>` tag, so it works straight from the folder (no `fetch()`). `xlsx.js` is a small built-in Excel writer for downloadable reports (no library).

Sample data for the proposed improvements and the wish-list screens sits in `data-extra.js` (loaded right after `data.js`), together with the [MOCK] data of JBAP's feedback of 19 Oct 2026 (new vehicles from inspection, earlier listings of four units) and of 20 Oct 2026 (the holiday list, rebate approval dates on two lots). The header switch **Proposed improvements** turns on LOGIQUE's proposals P2 to P6 (P1 was withdrawn after JBAP's feedback of 15 Oct 2026: JBAP keeps the Access format for Buyer Paid; P5 shrank on 20 Oct 2026, because JBAP made the SAAF standard: see *Proposed improvements*); a link ending in `?proposed=1` (for example `index.html?proposed=1`) opens with it on.

**Works in** Chrome, Edge, Firefox and Safari on Windows, macOS or Linux. If double-click does not open a browser: right-click `index.html` > Open With > choose a browser.

## What is clickable

- **Only Customer › Customer Inquiry, Report, FAD, SMD, VWD, Settings › Holiday List and Log Activity are active.** All other CDMS menus (Dashboard, Customer › Sales Actions, Cars, Payment, etc.) are disabled and show a short message when clicked.
- **38 screens**: Customer Inquiry 1, Report 5, FAD 17, SMD 9, VWD 4, Settings 1 (**Holiday List**, below Staff), Log Activity 1 (last item in the sidebar), plus a Home page (cards for FAD, SMD, VWD and Report, with counts per list) and a customer page per buyer. **All 38 screens show with the Proposed improvements switch off**: the SMD **Seller Awarding Acknowledgement** screen is standard since 20 Oct 2026 (JBAP answer Q11); it used to show only with the switch on (37 screens, 36 with the switch off). FAD's **Commission Billing**, **Billing Monitoring** and **Unpaid Billing** are three tabs of one **Billing** menu entry, **Rebate Monitoring** and **Rebate Payable** are two tabs of one **Rebate** menu entry, and SMD's **Return to Seller** and **SMD Manager Approval** are two tabs of one **Return to Seller** menu entry. **Report** holds **Result Summary**, **Auction Monitoring**, **Inventory Monitoring**, **Inventory Summary** and **Cancellation Monitoring**: Result Summary, Auction Monitoring and Cancellation Monitoring left FAD and SMD, and Inventory Monitoring left VWD and SMD (JBAP, 16 and 19 Oct 2026). There is no SMD Inventory and no VWD Inventory Monitoring any more. Old links to the moved screens still work and open the new screen.
- Every menu item in the sidebar shows its **number of records** (FAD › Billing, FAD › Rebate and SMD › Return to Seller show a count on each of their tabs instead; Inventory Summary counts its sellers; with the Proposed improvements switch on, the P6 badges replace the number; Log Activity has none).
- **New badge** (19 Oct 2026): everything added or changed since JBAP's feedback of 19 Oct 2026 carries a blue **New** badge: menu entries and Home links, page titles and group tabs, column headers, buttons and the Filter card of screens with dropdowns. Hover a badge for its meaning (*Added or changed after JBAP's feedback of 19 Oct 2026*). It is one badge, always on (not tied to the Proposed improvements switch; the amber **Proposed P#** tags stay separate), and it is hidden when printing. Changes of earlier rounds are not marked. The additions of 20 Oct 2026 (JBAP's answers to the 12 Oct open questions) carry the same badge and the same tooltip.
- **Dropdown filters are multi-select** (JBAP, 19 Oct 2026; JBAP wrote "radio type", we read it as tick boxes: Q83). A dropdown filter (Seller, Status, Parking Location …) shows **All** or the ticked values ("EWB, SBC"; "N selected" from three). Open it with a click, or Enter or Space on its button; tick one or more values; press **Apply filter** at the bottom of the dropdown (or Apply in the filter form); the same bar has **Reset**. **Escape**, tabbing out of the list or a click outside closes it (the ticks stay until you apply or reset); **Reset** clears every filter. A row passes when it matches **any** ticked value, and all filters together as before. Text and date-range filters are unchanged. A default such as Document Monitoring › Status = Open arrives already ticked. At 1366px or narrower an open dropdown can cover the form's own Reset and Apply buttons, so both are also inside the dropdown (Reset, then Apply filter). After Apply or Reset, keyboard focus returns to the dropdown's button, or to the form's Apply filter button when it was not in a dropdown.
- Every list has working **filters, column sorting, page size and paging**, an empty state, and totals where the design has them.
- **Dates can be typed** in every editable date cell and in the date fields of forms: `10/07/2026`, `10/07/26`, `10/07`, `1007`, `2026-10-07`, or **`t` for today** (the demo date). The calendar button next to the date still opens the date picker. A date that cannot be read stays in the box, turns red and is reported when you save. Filter date ranges keep the browser's picker.

## Flow walkthrough (FAD, data flows between lists and into VWD)

1. **Pending Awarding**: tick *For BAAF* on 1 or more lots (optionally change Awarding Date, Security Bond or **Rebate**) > **Email BAAF** > preview one email per buyer > **Send**. Lots move to **Unpaid**. The **Rebate** (JBAP, 19 Oct 2026; proposal Q73) is typed here, before the BAAF: 0 or more, not above the Result Price, blank = none. It is saved with Save changes or with the awarding, logged, and used by the BAAF's rebate attachment (the preview shows it before it is saved). **Rebate Approved (President)** (JBAP answer Q5, 20 Oct 2026) sits right after Rebate: a rebate has no set formula, SMD recommends it, the President approves it and FAD types the approval date here. The date is not in the future, not after the Awarding Date typed with it, and, when typed, not before the start of the lot's auction; it stays blank when the lot has no rebate (*No rebate to approve*). A lot with a rebate needs it before it is awarded: with an Awarding Date entered, Save changes says *The rebate needs the President's approval date before awarding.*, and **Email BAAF** holds such lots back with the same message (it stays above the list) and sends the others. The date is saved with the awarding, logged as *Rebate Approved (President)* and shown on Rebate Monitoring. Sample: V102 and V095 have a date; V092, V067, V074 and V082 have a rebate and no date yet. **Seller Approved** shows the date SMD recorded on the SAAF screen (Q11).
2. **Unpaid**: enter *Buyer Paid* (type `t` for today, the demo date, or use the calendar button; the row is marked unsaved) > **Save changes**. Lot moves to **Payment Seller**. Try a future date, or `13/45`, to see validation.
3. **Payment Seller**: tick *Print Payment to Seller* > **Record payment date & print** (JBAP, 19 Oct 2026, FAD #1). The dialog shows the report per seller and asks one **Payment date** (type it, or `t` for today; default today; required, not in the future, not before the latest Buyer Paid of the printed lots) > **Record payment date & print**. That date is recorded as *Payment to Seller* on every printed lot (and printed on the reports), the print ticks are cleared, any other unsaved edit on those rows (such as the Payment Voucher No. with the switch on) is saved, the changes are logged, and the browser's print dialog opens. The lots leave the list for **Seller Acknowledgement** and **Billing › Commission Billing**. A date typed in the *Payment to Seller Date* cell and saved still works for a lot that was not printed. Six sample lots (V128 to V133) start with the print tick set.
4. **Seller Acknowledgement**: enter the date and save. **For Dispatch** lists lots whose seller condition is met (Buyer Payment / DOAS sellers after Buyer Paid, others after Seller Acknowledgement): enter the date and save. **Document Monitoring** (JBAP's Documents Monitoring columns): enter the EWB, DOAS and document dates; Status and the two aging columns update by themselves, and the Open view hides Complete and Cancelled lots. Dispatched lots appear in **Unreleased Unit** (FAD), in SMD **Unreleased Unit** (the same live list, view only; JBAP, 19 Oct 2026, SMD #1) and in VWD **Unreleased Unit / Unreleased Unit (Overdue)**. VWD can then click **Release vehicle** on a row (see *Reports and emails*). A lot with a storage penalty cannot be released: on FAD **Unreleased Unit** press **Record payment** (optional Reference / OR No.). The payment is recorded as paid today, the dispatch deadline becomes today, the lot leaves VWD *Unreleased Unit (Overdue)* and can be released (JBAP, 16 Oct 2026, #9). After a payment the penalty starts again the next day, PHP 500 a day, until the unit is released (Q84). **Extend** (a button on every row; proposal Q74, 19 Oct 2026) opens a dialog with the customer, the current deadline and the penalty due, and asks for a typed **New deadline** (after the current deadline), a **Reason** and **Approved by (FAD Manager)**, all required. The new date becomes the dispatch deadline, so the penalty is recalculated from it and VWD sees the lot in the right list; the change is logged (Dispatch Deadline, Extension Reason, Extension Approved By) and kept in the lot's `deadline_changes`.
5. **Billing › Commission Billing** (columns Seller, Auction No. and Auction Date; JBAP, 16 and 19 Oct 2026, Q80): tick *Print?* for lots of **one seller** > **Print commission billing** > enter NOAH Billing No. and date > **Record billing & print**. A new billing appears in the *Billing Monitoring* and *Unpaid Billing* tabs.
6. **Billing › Unpaid Billing**: enter Payment Date + Collection Receipt > **Save**.
7. **Cancellation Entry**: enter an Auction No. and Lot (tip: copy one from Unpaid) > **Find** > set Status to *Cancelled*, date, waiver and reason > **Cancel transaction**. The list under the form shows every cancellation (filters: Auction No., Customer Name / Buyer ID, Plate Number, Waived) with its billing and payment dates. If the fee is not waived, the lot also appears in **Cancellation Billing & Payment**: tick *For Print?* > **Print cancellation billing**, then enter *Date Paid* (Amount is filled in) > **Save**; paid lots leave that list. Cancellation Payment no longer exists (JBAP, 16 Oct 2026, #13, #14), and Cancellation Monitoring is now **Report › Cancellation Monitoring** (step 13).
8. **Rebate › Rebate Payable**: enter *Rebate Paid* > **Save**. **Rebate › Rebate Monitoring** > **Download report**: pick From and To auction > **Download .xlsx**. **Liability to Seller**: pick a date > **Generate**.
9. **Log Activity**: every change from steps 1 to 8 (including *Penalty Paid*, the new dispatch deadline and, for an extension, its reason and approver), every VWD release or return, every SMD Return to Seller clearance and every SMD and FAD Manager approval, every SAAF sent or approved, and every holiday added or removed in Settings appears here as user `system`, one row per changed field with the old and new value (newest first). Filter by date, user, module, action or record/plate. Prints and emails that record a value (Awarding Date and Rebate, Payment to Seller Date, Billing #, Transmittal Date, Dispatch Date, cancellation billing date and amount, vehicle release with type and checklist, documents received with Others/Remarks and ATF No.) are logged; previews that change nothing and *Generate* are not.
10. **Return to Seller** (SMD, wish 4): the list holds units with status *Not Sold* or *Cancelled* that are **not listed in an ongoing or future auction** (JBAP, 16 Oct 2026, #19; 17 units in the sample). The column **Number of Listing** (JBAP, 19 Oct 2026, SMD #2) shows how often the unit was listed: the auction lots with its plate, plus earlier listings outside the data ([MOCK] `prior_listings` on 4 units), plus 1 when it has been relisted. Tick *For Return* > **Print & email clearance form** > **Send & print** (the form's sign lines read *Approved in CDMS*). The SMD Manager and the FAD Manager then approve in CDMS (proposal Q76, 19 Oct 2026): the units wait in **SMD › Return to Seller › SMD Manager Approval** (type the *SMD Manager Approved* date; not in the future, not before the clearance), then in **FAD › Return to Seller Approval** (type the *FAD Manager Approved* date; not in the future, not before the SMD approval); each approval is logged. Only FAD-approved units move to VWD **For Return to Seller** (with an *Approved by FAD Manager* column) > **Release vehicle** (type *Return to Seller*; the return date cannot be before the later of the clearance and the FAD approval). Sample: [MOCK] auction 222 (12 to 14 Oct 2026) holds three relisted units, which stay off the list.
11. **Report › Inventory Monitoring** (JBAP, 19 Oct 2026, VWD #4; replaces VWD *Inventory Monitoring* and SMD *Inventory*; wish 5, JBAP 16 Oct 2026, #2, #20, #23): **every unit in the yard**, one row each, for all departments: the **auction lots**, the **57 units of JBAP's parking file** (as of 12 Oct 2026) that are in the yard and are not lots, and **3 new vehicles from inspection** ([MOCK] sample; the real CDMS takes them from the Inspection module, which is not in the prototype). Columns: Date of Entry, VCN, Plate Number, CS No., Make/Model, Color, Year, Seller, Logistics, Originating Warehouse, Parking Location, Transfer Date, **First Auction Listed**, **End Date of Listing**, Auction Status, Inventory Status, Released to Buyer, Returned to Seller, Returned to Seller WH, Days in Yard. Filters (dropdowns are multi-select): *In yard / Out* (default In yard), *Auction Status*, *Inventory Status*, *Seller* (with *Not recorded*), *Parking Location* (OPEN, STEEL, IMET, *Not recorded*) and *Plate Number*; the *Latest auction* filter is gone. **View only**: *Inventory Status* is always calculated, first match wins: Released (Returned for a return to the seller), **For Return** (cleared by the SMD, even before the manager approvals), **For Release** (dispatched, waiting for the buyer's pull-out), **Awarded** (sold), **Listed** (the lot's own auction, or the auction it was relisted in, has not ended) and otherwise **Not Listed**; they show in the order Listed, Not Listed, Awarded, For Release, For Return, Released, Returned (JBAP's set, 4a to 4c). A unit from JBAP's file shows its file status (LISTED is Listed, FOR RETURN is For Return); a new inspection unit is Not Listed. The statuses Pending, For Next Auction and Storage and the VWD remarks are not built: JBAP's 19 Oct feedback replaced them with Listed / Not Listed (Q71, Q72). Yard and inspection units leave the auction columns blank.
12. **Security Bond** (FAD, JBAP feedback of 15 Oct 2026): lots with a bond appear once paid. Enter the ORCR and refund dates, Refund Via, or Forfeited. Tip: enter JBAP Receipt of Documents and Buyer Receipt of Documents for Auction 220, Lot 119 (plate NYL9684) in Document Monitoring, any date from 10/04 to 10/08/2026, to see *Waiting ORCR*.
13. **Report** (JBAP, 16 and 19 Oct 2026): **Result Summary** (Access snapshot, auctions 197 to 220), **Auction Monitoring** (live lots, with totals), **Inventory Monitoring** (step 11), **Inventory Summary** and **Cancellation Monitoring**. View, filter, sort only. **Inventory Summary** (JBAP's main objective, 19 Oct 2026; proposal Q70, like the REPORT sheet of JBAP's parking file) counts the units in the yard, one row per seller: In Yard, Listed, Not Listed, Awarded, For Release and For Return, with a Total row. The *Parking Location* filter chooses the units counted (for example only OPEN PARKING); units without a seller are grouped as *Not recorded*. It reads the same rows as Inventory Monitoring, so the counts follow every change made in FAD, SMD and VWD. **Cancellation Monitoring** (proposal Q69) lists every cancellation, newest first: Auction No., Lot, Customer Name, Buyer ID, Plate Number, Seller, Date Cancelled, Waived?, Justification, Penalty, Billing Date, Date Paid and Cancellation Fee Remitted; filters Auction No., Customer Name / Buyer ID, Plate Number, Seller and Waived; totals the count and the penalty. Old links to FAD and SMD Cancellation Monitoring and to VWD and SMD Inventory open the new screens.
14. **Customer Inquiry**: click a buyer to open the customer page. Its lot tabs are *Sold*, *Pending Awarding*, *Unpaid*, **Vehicle for release** (JBAP answer Q21, 20 Oct 2026: the buyer's sold lots with a Dispatch Date and no release yet, with the Sold columns plus Dispatch Date, Dispatch Deadline and Penalty), **Pending DOAS signature** (Receipt of DOAS filled, signed DOAS not back yet; since 19 Oct also **EWB lots that were called out and are not yet signed**, with an *EWB Call Out* column and *Working Days Since Receipt / Call Out*, counted from Receipt of DOAS, else from EWB Call Out), **Documents for release** (JBAP has the documents, the buyer does not yet) and *Cancellation* (JBAP, 16 Oct 2026; Q81, Q82). Sample: buyer B9034 has two EWB lots called out on 24 Aug 2026 and not signed (auction 211 lot 122, auction 210 lot 125). The *Sold* tab has a new column **Date of Release / Return** (the release date of the lot, any release type, blank while the unit is in the yard). Both are marked New. Sales action notes (our Q81, JBAP's Q22) and the bulk vehicle inquiry (JBAP's Q21) are not built, by decision.
15. **Settings › Holiday List** (JBAP answer Q14, 20 Oct 2026; a new top menu below Staff): the days CDMS skips when it counts working days. Columns Date, Day, Holiday and Type (*Regular holiday* or *Special non-working day*), sorted by date; filters Year and Type (multi-select). **Add holiday** above the list: a typed Date (like the other date fields), a Holiday name (required) and a Type; the date must not be listed yet. **Remove** on a row asks for a confirmation. Every add and remove is logged (module *Settings*; Log Activity's Module filter has Settings, and a removal is a *Delete*). The buyer, payment-to-seller and dispatch deadlines, the aging columns and the customer page's working days skip Saturdays, Sundays and every listed date at once: add a holiday inside a lot's 3 working days and its *Buyer Deadline* on Unpaid moves by a day; remove it and the deadline comes back. The 18 dates are [MOCK] sample data (fixed-date Philippine holidays, Nov 2025 to Dec 2026), to be kept by the CDMS administrator and checked against the official proclamation. In CDMS only users with the ADMIN role add or remove holidays; the prototype has no login, so everyone can, and the changes are lost on refresh.
16. **Proposed improvements**: turn on the switch in the header (see *Proposed improvements* below).

## Not simulated

- Persistence: all changes are lost on refresh (by design), including new Log Activity entries
- Real login: the header shows the CDMS user only, so every change made in the demo is logged as `system`. Log times use the demo date with the real clock time
- Real emails, NOAH and CDMS: email and print are previews; nothing is sent or posted. CDMS cancellations (P2) are sample data in `data-extra.js`
- NOAH: JBAP's existing integration is not connected; NOAH numbers are typed (P3, Q43)
- Partial penalty payments (Q75) and sales action notes (Q81): not built, they are questions for JBAP. A penalty payment is recorded for the full amount due, as of the demo date. The SMD and FAD Manager approvals are typed dates: with no sign-in, the Activity Log shows `system`, not the approver. The three new vehicles from inspection are sample data (the Inspection module is not in the prototype)
- Holiday list: the 18 dates are sample data, not an official list (moving holidays are missing); the ADMIN role is not simulated, so every visitor can add or remove holidays, and the changes are lost on refresh. The President's rebate approval is a typed date: with no sign-in the Activity Log shows `system`, not the approver
- Pixel-perfect design: this is a prototype, not the final UI
